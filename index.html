Glacier collapse and debris flow on the Rasuwa–Gyirong border

On 26 August 2026 a section of glacier detached at roughly 5,200 m and set off an ice and rock avalanche in the Lhende Khola catchment. The debris temporarily dammed the channel; when the blockage failed it released a flood wave down the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers, on both sides of the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region.
